# Lab 08 - Improvement Summary and Action Plan

## Objectives

- Summarize the improvement opportunity.
- Create a simple action plan.
- Identify who should be involved next.
- Present a White Belt improvement story.

## Scenario

You have observed a process, identified waste, collected simple data, and explored possible causes. You will now summarize the opportunity and prepare a practical next step.

## Steps

1. Review all lab worksheets.
2. Write the process name.
3. Identify the customer.
4. Summarize the problem in one sentence.
5. Summarize the waste observed.
6. Summarize the simple data collected.
7. Summarize the possible cause.
8. Write one practical improvement idea.
9. Identify the process owner or team that should review the idea.
10. Define the first action.
11. Assign an owner or recommended owner.
12. Set a due date or review date.
13. Define how the team will know whether the change helped.
14. Prepare a three-minute presentation.
15. Present to a peer or trainer and record feedback.

## Validation

- The summary uses evidence from previous labs.
- The action plan has owner, date, and measure.
- The proposed improvement is small and realistic.
- The presentation is clear and respectful.

## Deliverables

- Improvement summary.
- Action plan.
- Measurement idea.
- Presentation notes.
- Lessons learned.

## Review Questions

1. Why should an action plan include an owner?
2. Why should a change have a measure?
3. What makes an improvement idea realistic?
4. How can White Belts support continuous improvement after training?
