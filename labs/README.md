# Labs — Certified Lean Six Sigma White Belt (CLSSWB) Training

**WSQ Course Code:** TGS-2025053210  |  **Version v3 · 23 September 2026**

These labs follow the DMAIC roadmap end to end. Every lab builds on the same BrewBean Cafe morning rush scenario, so your outputs accumulate into one complete improvement package.

## Lab types

- **Core** — completed by everyone; maps directly to the assessment.
- **Elective** — additional practice with further Lean Six Sigma tools; run when time allows or after the course.

## What is in each lab folder

| Item | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `README.md` | The lab worksheet — objective, scenario, steps and the check |
| `data/` | The mock datasets as `.csv`, plus one `.xlsx` workbook holding every dataset and template on its own tab |
| `data/README.md` | Data dictionary — what each column means |
| `templates/` | Blank worksheets to fill in, as `.csv` |
| `model-answer.md` | The worked model answer — read it *after* you attempt the lab |
| `facilitator-notes.md` | Trainer copy: timing, what to watch for, common mistakes, debrief |

## About the data

Every figure is **mock data** built for training. It is internally consistent across all five labs:

- The Lab 2 observation log is the evidence base for the Lab 3 Pareto.
- The Lab 3 root cause is what the Lab 4 countermeasure addresses.
- The Lab 4 pilot results carry into the Lab 5 monitoring data.

So the numbers you quote in one lab still hold in the next, and the whole story reconciles at the end.
