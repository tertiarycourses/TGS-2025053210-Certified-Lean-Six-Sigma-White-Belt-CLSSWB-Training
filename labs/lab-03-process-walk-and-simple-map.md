# Lab 03 - Process Walk and Simple Map

## Objectives

- Observe a process respectfully.
- Record actual work steps.
- Draw a simple process map.
- Identify handoffs and waiting.

## Scenario

Processes often work differently from how people describe them. You will observe the real workflow and map what actually happens.

## Steps

1. Review the process from Lab 02.
2. Visit the place where work happens or interview someone who performs the work.
3. Observe without blaming, interrupting, or correcting people.
4. Record the actual steps in order.
5. Record who performs each step.
6. Note handoffs between people, teams, tools, or systems.
7. Note waiting points.
8. Note rework, repeated checking, or unclear decisions.
9. Draw a simple flowchart using boxes and arrows.
10. Add decision diamonds where the process branches.
11. Mark one process step that should be discussed with the process owner.
12. Ask another learner whether your map is easy to follow.

## Validation

- The map follows the actual process sequence.
- Handoffs and waits are visible.
- Observations are factual and respectful.
- The map can be understood by someone outside the process.

## Deliverables

- Process walk notes.
- Simple process map.
- Handoff and waiting notes.
- Process owner discussion point.

## Review Questions

1. What is the value of observing work directly?
2. Why should process walks avoid blame?
3. How can handoffs create problems?
4. What makes a process map useful?
