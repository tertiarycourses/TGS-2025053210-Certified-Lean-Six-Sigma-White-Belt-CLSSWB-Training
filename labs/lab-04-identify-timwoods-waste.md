# Lab 04 - Identify TIMWOODS Waste

## Objectives

- Recognize the eight wastes.
- Find waste in a simple process.
- Link waste to customer impact.
- Prioritize one waste area.

## Scenario

Lean improvement starts with seeing waste. You will review your process map and identify where time, effort, information, or talent is being wasted.

## Steps

1. Review TIMWOODS:
   - Transport.
   - Inventory.
   - Motion.
   - Waiting.
   - Overproduction.
   - Overprocessing.
   - Defects.
   - Skills.
2. Open the process map from Lab 03.
3. Review each process step one by one.
4. Mark examples of unnecessary movement of items or information.
5. Mark queues, backlog, or work-in-progress.
6. Mark unnecessary movement by people.
7. Mark waiting time.
8. Mark work done too early or in too much quantity.
9. Mark extra checking or repeated entry.
10. Mark errors, rework, missing information, or corrections.
11. Mark places where people's ideas or skills are not used.
12. Choose the one waste area with the largest visible impact.

## Validation

- Waste examples are tied to specific process steps.
- The priority waste has a clear customer or business impact.
- Observations are specific enough for discussion.
- The team avoids blaming individuals.

## Deliverables

- TIMWOODS waste table.
- Marked process map.
- Priority waste statement.
- One improvement question.

## Review Questions

1. Which waste is easiest to see in your process?
2. Which waste is easiest to overlook?
3. How can defects create other wastes?
4. Why is underused skill a waste?
